About The CCT

We are an organization focused on providing students hands on exposure to enterprise grade technology. At the CCT you will have access to three network stacks with all the infrastructure you need to experiment and learn new technologies. Additionally we host hands on workshops that are focued on preparing students for the workforce through exposure of in demand technical skills. Students of all majors, experience, and grade level are welcome.
